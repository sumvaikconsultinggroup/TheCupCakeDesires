FAQ

Quick answers for corporate orders

What’s the difference between standard, mini, and slices?

Standard cupcakes are full-size with edible logos (from 12). Minis are bite-size for networking trays (from 24). Cake slices are premium plated flavours for meetings and catering (from 12).

Can we put our logo on every cupcake?

Yes — upload your logo on the product page. We print edible toppers for corporate and mini orders. Slice boxes can include logo toppers on request.

How far in advance should we order?

Most boxes need 1–2 days’ notice. Larger event runs may need 5–7 days — book early for peak weeks. Checkout shows the earliest date for your basket.

Do you deliver across Melbourne?

We hand-deliver selected Greater Melbourne postcodes. Enter your postcode at checkout for the fee — free delivery on orders $100+.
